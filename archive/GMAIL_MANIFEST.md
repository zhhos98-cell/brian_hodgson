### 4. 2025 CV: `Paper Empires`

- Gmail message ID: `197730714010c0c7`
- Date: 2025-06-15
- Context: CV sent to JHI Blog after Graduate Student Symposium submission.
- Attachment: `CV of Haohao ZHANG.pdf`

The CV contains a dedicated project entry:

**Paper Empires: Material Infrastructures and Brian Hodgson's Epistemic Politics of Colonial Natural History**

It describes the archive base as Kew Garden Archive, Natural History Museum, Royal Asiatic Society, and British Library, and states an existing hypothesis about differentiated paper supports: British machine-made wove paper including Whatman/Towgood, Nepali handmade lokta, Calcutta machine paper, and administrative blue paper. It proposes that such distinctions materially shaped authenticity, bureaucratic authority, localized knowledge, circulation, and legitimation.

This is the principal earlier framing to retain and develop.

- Gmail message ID: `19efeb0ccdef4da0`
- Sender: SHNH Communications
- Subject: `New Virtual Issue (Free) The History of the Zoological Society of London (ZSL)`

The circular includes David A. Lowther, “The art of classification: Brian Houghton Hodgson and the ‘Zoology of Nipal’” (2019, *Archives of Natural History* 46(1): 1-23). This source is directly relevant to the scientific-history reframing and should be followed into the ZSL archive and the Lowther article.

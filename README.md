# Brian Houghton Hodgson — history of science working repository

This repository reconstructs Brian Houghton Hodgson as a problem in the history of science and material knowledge infrastructures. It does not begin from a fixed disciplinary hierarchy. Buddhist manuscripts, zoological specimens and drawings, geographical and survey papers, administrative records, paper technologies, and Hodgson's later household/personal archive are treated as distinct classes of knowledge objects whose argumentative weight is determined by evidence density.

## Core question

**How were heterogeneous Himalayan knowledge objects made credible, identifiable, countable, portable, auditable after separation, and institutionally usable?**

The project follows the concrete operations by which objects and claims moved: paper selection and procurement, copying, drawing, tabulation, annotation, collation, numbering, wrapper and title-slip production, binding and bundling, packing, shipment, receipt, unpacking, counting, segmentation, comparison, sorting, cataloguing, redistribution, rebinding, relabelling, re-identification, delayed publication, correction, reprinting, and retrospective archival reorganisation.

## Main analytical axes

1. **Object-making and auditability** — how numbers, labels, slips, foliation, drawings, lists, copy relations, receipts and catalogues allowed physically separated objects to be reconstructed, compared and authenticated, and how failures produced later problems of identity, provenance, sequence and priority.
2. **Paper technologies and material affordances** — Nepali handmade paper, British and Indian machine/wove papers, watermarks, sheet formats, wrappers, tickets, copying paper, drawing supports and other paper regimes studied through historically specific performance and use rather than a predetermined symbolic hierarchy.
3. **Containers, transport and custody** — bastas, bundles, trunks, boxes, crates, portfolios, rolls and parcels as devices that protected objects, preserved or rearranged relations, crossed transport systems, and generated custody records. Transport unit and disciplinary unit are not assumed to coincide.
4. **Conversion across media and knowledge forms** — specimen + ticket + drawing + description; manuscript + wrapper + title slip; image + diagnostic accessory + European identifier; testimony + compiler + notebook + private print. These are assembled evidence systems rather than naturally given units.
5. **Institutional handling and evidential afterlives** — receipt, unpacking, counting, segmentation, expert identification, sorting, comparison, relabelling, rebinding, delayed opening, redistribution, publication gating and cataloguing are treated as further epistemic stages, not passive preservation.
6. **Recursive archive, household labour and reputation** — Anne/Annie Hodgson's testimonial compilation, Brian Hodgson's recovery and re-inscription of household evidence, and his later corrections, lists, private prints and renewed copies are treated as practices of evidential assembly, revision, priority-making and career re-segmentation.
7. **Changing verification regimes** — the standing of a medium or descriptive unit could change even when the material survived: drawings could be downgraded when photography became preferred evidence, and physical codices could be re-segmented into different numbers of textual works after deeper inspection.
8. **Intermediate-state fixation** — working annotations, provisional classifications, copy states and delayed publication decisions can become durable institutional or printed states. The same persistence that makes later reconstruction possible can also stabilize unfinished, inconsistent or context-stripped knowledge.

## Working title

**Paper Empires: Brian Houghton Hodgson and the Material Infrastructures of Himalayan Knowledge**

If the completed census shows that natural history carries substantially more evidential weight than the other corpora, a narrower natural-history title can be restored. The scope follows the archive rather than being fixed in advance.

## Buddhism policy for this repo

Buddhist doctrinal historiography does not determine the argument. Buddhist manuscripts, drawings and catalogues themselves are not reduced to contextual evidence: they remain central wherever they expose copying industries, paper and copy states, wrappers and foliation, authentication, collation, explanatory text-image relations, packing, receipt, institutional custody, catalogue error, segmentation failure, diagnostic abstraction, publication gating or other material mechanisms comparable with natural-history and administrative objects.

The same rule applies across domains. A source is retained because it exposes a knowledge-making operation, not because it belongs to a preferred modern discipline.

## Current high-value mechanisms

- **Material affordance:** paper/support choice must be demonstrated through use and performance, not assigned a fixed epistemic meaning in advance.
- **Affordance reversal:** a property can change from advantage to defect across operations or technical interfaces. Darjeeling/Serampore paper trials show toughness-associated fibre behaviour helping hand-formed sheets while obstructing a machine web, and shifting again in value across filtration and waterproof packing.
- **Relational preservation:** a container can preserve associations among drawings, labels, manuscripts, lists and specimens even while blocking access; active cataloguing can enable use while destroying those relations.
- **Interoperable load unit:** Hodgson's commerce writing explicitly designs parcel weight around transfer between bullocks and one-man mountain carriage, reducing repacking at transport interfaces.
- **Custody checksum:** `safe arrival` acknowledgements confirm only the unit actually counted at a custody transition; they leave internal completeness, segmentation, identity, unpacking and catalogue incorporation to later stages.
- **Receipt and condition state:** Kew's Inwards Book provides an archive-side example of an arrival being converted into local metadata keyed to date, sender, origin and contents, while neighbouring entries show that condition/survival can also be recorded. Physical receipt and later scientific usability are distinct states.
- **Container chronology:** dates and notes on boxes, cases, packets or wrappers may record packing, repacking or storage events rather than the production or first shipment of their contents; later catalogues can misdate objects when those layers are collapsed.
- **Counting ontology:** leaves, copies, works, physical volumes, bundles, packages, boxes, catalogue entries, drawing sheets and represented subjects are different operational count units. Counts across unit classes are not reconciled unless a historical conversion relation is demonstrated.
- **Institutional legibility:** physical receipt does not guarantee institutional usability. Access, segmentation, authorised expertise, language/script, classification and descriptive fields determine whether received objects can be identified and operated on.
- **Future-operation reserve:** some knowledge objects are materially designed for work not yet completed. A later India Office description of Hodgson's Lepcha copies identifies blank right-hand space reserved for future translation, with Nepalese translation already inserted in places.
- **Provisional classification surface:** the 1838 mammal catalogue and 1857 ethnographic vocabularies explicitly preserve classifications that Hodgson treats as useful but revisable. Classification is an operational state, not necessarily a final taxonomy.
- **Intermediate-state fixation:** temporary working states can acquire durability through copying, transfer or print. Hodgson later explained that hints for correction/addition, meant to be erased, entered the 1857 ethnographic print state; Wave 24 independently shows a retained zoological copy preserving incompatible counts.
- **Diagnostic feature schema and abstraction:** by 1827 Hodgson explicitly distinguished Buddhist images through mudra/hand position, supporters, cognizances and colour. By 1860 diagnostic symbols were detached from temples, statues and pictures into a portable comparative corpus.
- **Access-dependent evidence production:** Buddhist visual and textual evidence reached Hodgson through differentiated roles including a Vajra Acharya, a Buddhist Chitrakar, a Bhotiya image provider and Hodgson's own textual checking. Local actors should be recorded by operation rather than collapsed into one `informant` category.
- **Publication gatekeeping and recipient buffering:** Henry Yule retained Hodgson's 1870 letter and original sketch for roughly two years while waiting for a less isolated publication context, then submitted them to the Royal Asiatic Society in 1872. Circulation latency can be epistemic/editorial rather than transport-based.
- **Segmentation failure:** material boundaries can be mistaken for intellectual/object boundaries. Mitra's 1882 account shows continuously paginated multi-work codices collapsed into one catalogue identity when only first and last pages were sampled, while the reverse error could split one work under multiple names.
- **Error-control redundancy:** repeated numbers, labels, lists, distribution tables, receipts, paired representations and later comparative catalogues are treated as material systems for controlling identity across large distributed collections.
- **Reconciliation surface:** lists, catalogues and tables can compare expected, received, retained, distributed, missing and corrected states. Wave 24 shows this explicitly in the 1844–45 zoological distribution sequence; archive ingestion supplies further checksum targets.
- **Household-to-print reputation pipeline:** Anne/Annie Hodgson compiled testimonial material from handwritten copies and cuttings; Brian found at least one such notebook in her desk in March 1868. The later privately printed `Notes of the Services`, strongly attributed to Hodgson himself despite its `Collected by a Friend` voice, repeatedly classified, expanded, annotated and renewed career evidence.
- **Career re-segmentation:** the expanded `Notes of the Services` state sorts external judgments into vernacular education, Hindu law/legal administration, physical geography, ethnology, European settlement/trans-Himalayan commerce, Buddhism and zoology. Reputation itself becomes a versioned catalogue object.

## Repository principle

This is a research reconstruction repository, not an email dump or a thematic scrapbook. Provenance should be preserved; duplicated materials and checked-but-irrelevant sources should be excluded or explicitly marked. Claims should remain source-level and mechanism-level wherever possible, with later historiographical synthesis built from the evidence rather than substituted for it. When a later source-control pass corrects an earlier inference, the correction should remain explicit rather than silently rewriting the research history.

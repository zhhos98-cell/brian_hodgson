# Web research 2026-09-01 — Wave 52
## Phoenix to 1874: transmission direction without direct Phoenix micro-reading

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: paper-centred publication-versioning follow-up. This wave narrows the 1857 -> Phoenix 1872 -> Essays 1874 transmission chain using a near-contemporary review while preserving the direct-reading gap for Phoenix No.26 pp.31–35.

## Executive result

A 1875 review in *The Indian Antiquary* independently states that the 1874 volume reprints papers **as first reprinted in the Phoenix**, and that the Phoenix versions incorporated numerous marginal notes from Hodgson's own copies of the earlier *Illustrations* and *Selections* into the text.

This materially sharpens the transmission direction:

`1857 printed Selections`
-> `Hodgson manuscript marginalia on his own printed copy`
-> `Phoenix serial reprint with marginal notes introduced into text`
-> `1874 collected Essays base text`
-> `1874 Additions and Corrections apparatus`
-> `post-1874 copy-specific manuscript annotation branches`.

Mechanisms:

- `PRINTED_COPY_REOPENS_AS_MANUSCRIPT_SURFACE`
- `MARGINALIA_ENTER_SERIAL_PRINT`
- `SERIAL_REPRINT_FEEDS_COLLECTED_EDITION`
- `EDITION_BASE_AND_CORRECTION_APPARATUS_ARE_SEPARATE_VERSION_STATES`
- `INDIRECT_TRANSMISSION_EVIDENCE_DOES_NOT_EQUAL_DIRECT_READING`.

## 1. 1875 Indian Antiquary review

Text source:
https://jainqq.org/booktext/Indian_Antiquary_Vol_04_Romanized/032496

The review says that the anonymous editor of Hodgson's 1874 volume explains that the articles are reprints of papers as first reprinted in the *Phoenix*, consisting of the earlier essays from *Illustrations* and *Selections* **with numerous marginal notes introduced into the text from Hodgson's own copies**.

The review further says that, apart from eight pages completing the commerce paper, the 1874 volume reproduced the papers that had appeared in the *Phoenix*.

This evidence is near-contemporary and independent of the modern bibliography used to locate Phoenix No.26.

## 4. Material implication

This sequence is a paper-versioning event, not merely an abstract textual history.

The same proposition therefore crosses handwriting and typography repeatedly.

## Bottom line

The direct Phoenix micro-reading remains open, but the transmission history is now substantially tighter. A near-contemporary 1875 review independently confirms that the 1874 collected text was built from the Phoenix reprints, and that the Phoenix stage had already converted Hodgson's marginal manuscript notes on earlier printed copies into running print. The most likely current sequence is therefore not `1857 -> 1874 revision`, but `1857 print -> manuscript-on-print -> Phoenix serial print -> 1874 collected print -> correction apparatus`, with Phoenix as the remaining word-level breakpoint.

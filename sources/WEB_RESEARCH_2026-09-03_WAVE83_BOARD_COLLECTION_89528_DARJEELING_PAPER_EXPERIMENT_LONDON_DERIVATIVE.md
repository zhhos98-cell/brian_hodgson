Date: 2026-09-03

## 4. Board's Collection changes the recovery architecture

Earlier model:

`underlying Campbell letter/report`
-> `Political Original Consultation`
-> `Political Proceedings`
-> `[possible Political Letter to London]`
-> `L/PS/6 / E/4`.

Wave 83 requires a parallel branch:

`underlying Campbell letter/report`
-> `Political consultation / proceedings`
-> `[selection/transmission to Court/Board]`
-> `Board's Collection IOR/F/4/2006/89528`.

Boards Collections are especially important because they can preserve enclosures or documentary packets that are absent from the received-letter volumes.

Thus retrieval should no longer treat `F/4` as merely a fallback after `L/PS/6`. For the Campbell experiment, an exact subject-level F/4 file is already known.

Mechanism:

`KNOWN_F4_SUBJECT_FILE_CAN_PRECEDE_UNKNOWN_TRANSMISSION_LETTER_IN_RETRIEVAL_ORDER`.

## 5. Darjeeling paper is embedded in a larger station-proceedings file

`IOR/F/4/2006/89528` does not isolate paper as a standalone technological dossier. Its scope also covers:

- pay of a Mohaner supervising Bhutan-frontier boundary marks;
- commencement of the Birch Hill road using station funds;
- progress of public works at Darjeeling in Jan–Feb 1842;
- Campbell's paper experiment report.

This suggests the paper experiment entered London archival organization as one component of a broader Darjeeling administrative packet.

Do not infer from this alone that paper manufacture was funded from the same station funds or administratively classified as Public Works.

What is secure is:

`PAPER_EXPERIMENT_REPORT_COLOCATED_WITH_DARJEELING_STATION_PROCEEDINGS`.

This may explain why broad searches for `paper` in Political-letter series have been weak: the report can be recoverable under the locality/administrative packet rather than under a dedicated paper subject heading.

Mechanisms:

- `LOCALITY_PACKET_CAN_HIDE_TECHNOLOGY_REPORT`
- `ARCHIVAL_COLOCATION_IS_NOT_BUDGETARY_OR_DEPARTMENTAL_IDENTITY`.

2. The file is dated Mar–Nov 1842 and belongs to Board's Collections.
3. Treat it as a direct London documentary state of the same broad Campbell/Darjeeling paper programme.

11. The 25 Mar Hodgson pulp consignment remains materially unlinked to Campbell's paper experiment.

## Finding 26 — Abdul Ali's `Political O.C.` is an Original Consultation retrieval address, not a generic citation

Evidence status: **SECURE archival-method control.**

Contemporary/later handbooks to the Imperial Record Department explain the record architecture of pre-Mutiny Government of India consultations:

- councils met on particular dates and transacted business item by item;
- each document/business item considered at that consultation was assigned a sequential number;
- the original incoming letter, orders passed on it, and draft/copy of reply were preserved as an **Original Consultation (O.C.)**;
- the date + consultation number therefore identify a specific item within a specific council sitting;
- separate **Proceedings** volumes copied the transaction into bound folio registers;
- proceedings do not always reproduce all enclosures, so original consultations can preserve important material absent from the proceedings copy.

Archival handbook evidence:
- Douglas Dewar, *A Hand-book to the English Pre-Mutiny Records in the Government Record Rooms of the United Provinces of Agra and Oudh* (1920), discussion of Political Consultations / Original Consultations;
- Government of India records handbook lists Original Consultations alongside proceedings and diaries.

### Consequence for Abdul Ali's citations

`Political O.C., 11 May 1842, no.70` should be operationalised as:

**Political Original Consultation — 11 May 1842 — item no.70**

not as a broad file title or an approximate subject reference.

Likewise:
- `Political O.C., 29 Nov 1841, no.147` = a precise original-consultation item;
- `Pol. O.C., 13 Jan 1832, no.19` = a precise original-consultation item associated by Abdul Ali with Hodgson's 8 Dec 1831 letter / paper specimens.

## Finding 28 — NAI search strategy should now be archival-address first, keyword second

National Archives of India officially holds Foreign Department records covering the Hodgson period. Abhilekh Patal provides structured metadata for millions of records and Digitize on Demand for records represented in the database but not yet digitized; records known only through reference tools can be pursued through the NAI Research Room.

Current web environment cannot execute Abhilekh Patal's JavaScript search interface reliably, so failure to return No.70 here is **not evidence that the item is absent**.

When searching/requesting at NAI, use the archival address before subject keywords:

- branch/series: Political / Foreign Department pre-Mutiny consultation records;
- record type: Original Consultation;
- date: 11 May 1842;
- item: no.70;
- names: Archibald Campbell; G. A. Bushby;
- subject fallback: Darjeeling; paper manufacture; Daphne; Military Board; Stationery Department.

For 1841 bleaching:

For Hodgson's 1831 intervention:

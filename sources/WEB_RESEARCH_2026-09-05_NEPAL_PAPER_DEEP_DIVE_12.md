Publicly indexed scholarship gives page-level references into the Stiller Typescript, including:

- Campbell, `Notes on the Agriculture and Rural Economy of the Valley of Nipal`, 20 February 1837, cited to Stiller Typescript Reel Three, Vol. 2 (June 1973), around p.220.

These citations confirm that the typescript can support document-level navigation once the relevant volume/reel is in hand. They do **not** establish a simple chronological pagination sequence across the whole transcription. January 1832 therefore must not be assigned a guessed page by interpolation from February 1833 or later Campbell references.

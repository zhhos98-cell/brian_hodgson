`sources/WEB_RESEARCH_2026-09-03_WAVE79_POLITICAL_OC_ORIGINAL_COLLECTION_AND_CITATION_DATE_SPLIT.md`

Wave 79 remains valid for its direct recovery of Abdul Ali's 1944 article, its proof that underlying-letter dates differ from `Political O.C.` dates, its subject control of the 1841/42 targets, and its downgrade of date-interpolated London mirrors.

One interpretation is superseded by Wave 80:

`Political O.C. = Political Original Collection`

should no longer be used for Abdul Ali's pre-Mutiny Foreign/Political citations.

Current controlled reading:

`Political O.C. = Political Original Consultation`.

Reason: Douglas Dewar's 1920 archival handbook explicitly describes original received letters, orders and drafts/copies of replies as `Original Consultations`, explains the consultation-date/item-number system, and lists `Political Consultations, 1790–1859` in the Imperial Record Department. Qeyamuddin Ahmad's NAI-based archival scholarship independently gives the explicit abbreviation `O.C. — Original Consultation`, while modern NAI citations use `Political Consultations + date + no./nos.` in the same form as Abdul Ali.

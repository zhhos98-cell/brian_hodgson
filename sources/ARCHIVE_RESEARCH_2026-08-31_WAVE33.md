# Archive research wave 33 — privately financed, bureaucratically embedded: the fiscal and administrative status of Hodgson's research establishment

Date: 2026-08-31

Status: source-control reconstruction from archival project biographies, AIM25/ZSL descriptions, Cambridge inventory entries for British Library Hodgson Papers, and related collection descriptions. Exact payroll allocation remains unresolved; this wave is designed to prevent premature classification of the research staff as either purely private household labour or formal East India Company/Residency establishment.

## Executive result

The evidence supports a **hybrid research establishment**.

Two facts coexist:

1. authoritative archival biographies state that Hodgson retained a group of local research assistants **at his own expense**;
2. at least one central actor, Khardar Jitmohan, is described simultaneously as a **learned official of the British Residency** and Hodgson's **principal research assistant**.

The research system therefore crossed the boundary between private patronage/household expenditure and Residency administrative infrastructure.

The correct working formulation is:

`PRIVATELY_FINANCED_BUT_BUREAUCRATICALLY_EMBEDDED_RESEARCH_ESTABLISHMENT`.

This is a hypothesis about institutional position, not a claim that every assistant was privately paid or that every Residency official worked on Hodgson's research in an official capacity. Payment source must be reconstructed actor by actor.

---

## 1. Archival biographies explicitly state private financing

Both the Cambridge Hodgson Papers project introduction and the ZSL/AIM25 collection description state that Hodgson retained **at his own expense** a group of local research assistants and trained some of his staff as naturalists, especially in ornithology.

### Mechanism

Code:

`PRIVATE_PATRONAGE_OF_RESEARCH_STAFF`

### Source-control status

This is a modern archival-biographical statement based on the broader Hodgson record, not a substitute for an original payroll.

Use as high-quality orientation; verify through accounts before assigning payments to named individuals.

---

## 2. Principal research assistant Jitmohan is also institutionally embedded in the Residency

`MSS EUR HODGSON/79` describes Khardar Jitmohan as:

- a learned official of the British Residency;
- Hodgson's principal research assistant.

### Mechanism

Code:

`DUAL_ADMINISTRATIVE_RESEARCH_ROLE`

### Implication

The same person can occupy:

`Residency office role + scholarly/research production role`.

This prevents a simple separation between `colonial administration` and `scientific/ethnographic research` at the personnel level.

### Caution

The inventory description does not establish whether Jitmohan's research labour was paid from:

- Residency salary;
- Hodgson's private funds;
- mixed remuneration;
- informal expectations attached to office;
- separate payments.

That fiscal join remains unresolved.

---

## 3. `Khardar` should be treated as an office-status marker, not merely a personal label

Across the BL inventory, Jitmohan appears repeatedly as `Khardar Jitman/Jitmohan`.

His work includes:

- vocabulary/dictionary compilation;
- genealogy;
- mapping;
- Lepcha/Limbu compilation;
- title and colophon work;
- translations;
- coordination with local specialists.

### Mechanism

Code:

`OFFICIAL_STATUS_REPURPOSED_FOR_RESEARCH`

The research system can exploit capacities associated with administrative literacy, multilingualism, documentation and official access.

### Research question

Was the Residency office itself supplying documentary skills and access to Hodgson's scholarly programme, or was Hodgson privately mobilizing an official outside formal duties?

The archive can potentially distinguish these through orders, pay, office stationery, work hours, titles and account entries.

## 4. Direct accounting surfaces for money paid to helpers survive

### BL inventory witness

`MSS EUR HODGSON/56` includes **rough notes of account of money paid to helpers**, physically associated in the volume with:

- names and drawings of Tibetan animals;
- Tibetan family names;
- Jitmohan correspondence;
- other working materials.

### Mechanism

Code:

`HELPER_PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_SURFACE`

### Importance

This is closer to the fiscal infrastructure than general biographical statements.

The original folios may allow reconstruction of:

- named payee;
- amount;
- date;
- task;
- recurring versus one-off payment;
- relation to zoological / ethnographic / logistical labour.

### Current limit

The inventory does not expose the individual entries or payees. No actor-specific payment should yet be asserted from this description alone.

## 5. Household expense accounts preserve the lower labour infrastructure

### BL inventory witness

`MSS EUR HODGSON/58, ff.19–22`, 1857:

- kitchen and other expenses incurred by Lachuman/Laxman;
- names of Hodgson's regular paid workers (`kullis`).

### Mechanisms

- `HOUSEHOLD_EXPENSE_LEDGER`
- `REGULAR_PAID_WORKER_TRACE`

### Source-control distinction

The existence of regular paid workers is secure from the inventory.

Their relation to research is not.

A kuli may be:

- domestic labour;
- carrier;
- archive mover;
- specimen/logistics labour;
- some combination.

The folios are valuable precisely because they may show where household and research logistics overlap.

## 7. Scientific patronage and official office capacity interact

Hodgson's political career supplied:

- Residency location;
- administrative correspondence channels;
- multilingual officials/munshis/khardars;
- access to Nepal court/official records;
- social authority to commission information.

His private expenditure supplied at least some research-assistant labour according to archival biographies.

### Mechanism

Code:

`PRIVATE_PATRONAGE_PUBLIC_OFFICE_COUPLING`

### Analytical value

Instead of saying `colonial office enabled science` in the abstract, the article can test the specific interfaces:

`office -> personnel/access/document channels`

`private funds -> extra research capacity`

`household -> storage/logistics/accounting`

`learned societies -> publication/distribution`.

These interfaces are historically separable even when they overlap.

---

## 8. Research labour may continue after formal Residency service through a reconfigured establishment

Hodgson resigned official service in 1844, returned to India and settled in Darjeeling, but the later archive still preserves:

- Jitmohan-related work;
- Rajman work and bird-selection/shipment relations;
- regional assistants/sources;
- paid workers/household accounts;
- internal archive issuance records;
- standardized/documented research operations.

### Mechanism

Code:

`POST_OFFICE_RESEARCH_ESTABLISHMENT_CONTINUITY`

### Research question

Which personnel/operations migrate with Hodgson from Kathmandu Residency into Darjeeling private life, and which depend on formal office?

This transition can test the hybrid-establishment model directly.

---

## 9. Rajman is a critical continuity test

Rajman is documented as an artist associated with Hodgson across Nepal/Darjeeling and, in `vol.58 f.59b`, as a participant in bird selection/recommendation/shipment as well as drawing.

### Mechanism

Code:

`MOBILE_SKILLED_STAFF_CONTINUITY`

If Rajman's compensation, travel and task records can be recovered across the 1844 break, he could show how a Residency-associated scientific relationship was converted into a private Darjeeling research relationship.

### Current limit

No secure actor-level salary sequence has yet been reconstructed.

---

## 10. Jitmohan is the strongest test of administrative/research overlap

Because Jitmohan is explicitly described as both Residency official and principal research assistant, his papers can answer several institutional questions:

- Did his official title persist after Hodgson's resignation?
- Were his Darjeeling compilations done while formally employed by the Residency/government?
- Who supplied paper, scribal resources and travel?
- Was he paid separately for research tasks?
- Did he supervise other assistants?

### Mechanism

Code:

`ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL_RESEARCH_REPURPOSING`

This should be investigated through payroll/personnel records as well as Hodgson's private accounts.

## 11. The local research establishment is fiscally heterogeneous by design or circumstance

Current evidence points to several possible resource streams:

### A. Hodgson private expenditure

- archival biographies explicitly state private retention of assistants;
- household/helper payment accounts survive.

### B. Residency institutional salary/status

- Jitmohan as learned Residency official;
- Munshi/official translators and office personnel may enter research tasks.

### C. Specialist or commissioned piecework

- artists;
- shikarees;
- manuscript copyists;
- local ritual specialists;
- guides/traders;
- possible task-specific payment.

### D. Household/logistics labour

- kullis;
- carriers;
- packers;
- servants;
- archive handling.

### Mechanism

Code:

`FISCALLY_HETEROGENEOUS_RESEARCH_LABOUR`

No single payroll necessarily contains the whole knowledge infrastructure.

## 14. Hybrid status explains why modern disciplinary and institutional categories mislead

A person can be:

- a Residency official;
- a paid private assistant;
- a scholar/translator;
- a household-linked worker;
- a visual artist;
- a field collector;

at different times or simultaneously.

### General mechanism

`ROLE_AND_FUNDING_NONCOINCIDENCE`

The source categories `office`, `household`, `science`, `ethnography`, `art` and `logistics` do not map neatly onto separate groups of people.

This is an archival reason to reconstruct operations before institutional labels.

## 15. Revised organizational model

The local system is best represented as three overlapping infrastructures rather than one hierarchy.

### Administrative infrastructure

- Residency officials;
- Munshi/Khardar expertise;
- court/official access;
- official correspondence/document channels.

### Private research/patronage infrastructure

- assistants retained at Hodgson's expense;
- artists;
- field collectors;
- shikarees;
- commissioned copyists;
- specialist collaborators.

### Household/logistical infrastructure

- regular paid workers;
- expense manager(s);
- carriers/kullis;
- storage/issue control;
- domestic support enabling research continuity.

Actors may sit in more than one infrastructure.

## 16. Relationship to metropolitan institutions

The hybrid local establishment produces objects and documents that then enter a second labour regime:

`private/Residency/household mixed production`

`-> shipment`

`-> British Museum/ZSL/RAS sorting, naming, copying, catalogue and distribution`.

### Mechanism

Code:

`HYBRID_LOCAL_TO_FORMAL_METROPOLITAN_HANDOFF`

The contrast should not be framed simply as informal local knowledge versus formal metropolitan science.

The local side already contains formalized questions, official personnel, accounts, archive control and specialized labour.

1. Transcribe `MSS EUR HODGSON/56` money-paid-to-helpers entries completely.
2. Transcribe `MSS EUR HODGSON/58 ff.19–22` and recover names, payment amounts and categories of regular workers/kullis.
3. Search East India Company / Residency establishment lists for Jitmohan/Jitman, Munshi and related staff, including salary/title dates.
4. Search Hodgson private accounts for Rajman, Tursmoney, Jatanlal, Ashraf Ali and Jitmohan.
5. Track which staff persist from pre-1844 Kathmandu into post-1845 Darjeeling.
6. Distinguish retainer/salary from piecework and travel reimbursement.
7. Determine whether shikarees were paid per specimen, as retainers, or through other arrangements.
8. Identify who maintained the Darjeeling archive issue register.
9. Reconstruct cost of one complete zoological evidence package where possible: acquisition -> preparation -> drawing -> note/translation -> packing -> shipping.
10. Separate formal Residency duties from private research tasks at actor/event level.

## Bottom line

Hodgson's research organization should not be described as either a private gentleman's household or a colonial Residency office. The surviving evidence points to a mixed establishment: archival biographies say assistants were retained at Hodgson's own expense, yet Jitmohan appears simultaneously as a Residency official and principal research assistant; rough accounts record money paid to helpers; household books preserve regular paid workers; and research work continues after Hodgson leaves formal service. The important historical question is therefore how private money, official status, household logistics and learned-society destinations were coupled. Reconstructing that coupling turns `colonial knowledge` from an institutional label into a traceable labour and funding system.

# Hodgson paper mainline status — 2026-08-31

Status: active article mainline. This file records the paper-centred research state after Waves 23–30 so subsequent work does not drift back into generic Hodgson biography or a discipline-wide archive survey.

## Current paper-centred question

How did paper move through manufacture, procurement, storage, allocation, knowledge conversion and later custody, and how did those material transitions affect what Hodgson and his collaborators could write, draw, compare, translate, tabulate, transport and institutionalise?

## Current argument stack

### A. Production history

1833 Cowan trial versus successful Nepal-made finished sheets:

`same broad material source + different process history -> different paper properties`.

Core mechanism:

`PROCESS_HISTORY_EMBODIED_IN_SHEET`.

### B. Interface-relative affordance

Darjeeling and Serampore:

`property value = material property × technical interface × intended operation`.

Core mechanism:

`AFFORDANCE_REVERSAL`.

### C. Mixed-stock ecology

Hodgson-associated objects document Whatman, Joynson, Moinier's Patent, Newey/Darnford, Britannia and dated paper stocks alongside Nepalese handmade paper.

Core mechanisms:

- `HODGSON_OBJECT_LEVEL_IMPORTED_PAPER_PLURALITY`
- `WHATMAN_PRESENT_BUT_NOT_DEFAULT`
- `MIXED_STOCK_OPERATIONAL_ECOLOGY`.

### D. Stock time

Watermark date may substantially predate object-use date; paper can have a storage/recirculation history before research use.

Core mechanism:

`PAPER_STOCK_RETENTION_OR_REUSE`.

### E. Allocation by operation

Cambridge pilot suggests that paper support tracks documentary operation and actor/version layer more strongly than subject alone. Local source/copy layers often use Nepali handmade paper; analytical notebooks, tables, questionnaires and forwarding lists often use machine-made paper; composite dossiers often mix supports.

Core mechanism:

`PAPER_ALLOCATION_AS_RESEARCH_INFRASTRUCTURE`.

### F. Support conversion during knowledge conversion

Explicit source-to-translation/source-to-abstract pairs show content continuity across material support change.

Core mechanisms:

- `SUPPORT_SWITCH_AT_KNOWLEDGE_CONVERSION`
- `SOURCE_TO_ABSTRACT_MATERIAL_RECODING`
- `TRANSLATION_AS_SUPPORT_CONVERSION`
- `MULTISTAGE_SUPPORT_CONVERSION_CHAIN`.

### G. Post-use custody

Auction splitting, private/institutional custody and shelfmark drift determine present access to paper evidence.

Core mechanism:

`POST_RESEARCH_PAPER_CUSTODY_HISTORY`.

## Combined model

`paper-object history = production history × supply/stock history × allocation × conversion history × post-use custody`.

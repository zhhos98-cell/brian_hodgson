# Hodgson paper-history mainline — 2026-09-02, through Wave 66

Status: current active guardrail. Supersedes `PAPER_HISTORY_MAINLINE_STATUS_2026-09-02_WAVE63.md` for ongoing work. Waves 64–66 move the article from post-manufacture format conversion into **procurement, local acquisition channels, and support conversion across research workflows**. Publication/classification/person-network work remains parked unless it directly reveals a paper operation.

## Current object

The article is a history of paper as a repeatedly remade material unit:

`fibre/raw material`
-> `manufacturing process`
-> `commercial/local sheet or bundle`
-> `procurement/acquisition`
-> `stock/storage time`
-> `cutting/folding/trimming`
-> `working-format allocation`
-> `writing/drawing/copying`
-> `recopying onto newly allocated support`
-> `binding/bundling/packaging`
-> `transport/container`
-> `custody/rebinding`
-> `modern catalogue object`.

The paper object now carries at least six analytically distinct histories:

1. **process history** — how physical properties were made;
2. **supply history** — how the sheet/bundle entered Hodgson's working environment;
3. **stock-time history** — how long and where it was retained before use;
4. **format history** — how parent stock was cut/folded into a task unit;
5. **work history** — how information/images were written, recopied or transferred across supports;
6. **custody history** — how sheets were bound, packed, shipped, mounted, dispersed or rebound.

Core equation:

`research support = manufacture × acquisition channel × stock time × format conversion × task allocation × later custody`.

## 1. Manufacture remains the opening material experiment

### Cowan 1829/1833

Related Himalayan material moved as prepared/dried pulp did not reproduce the properties of successful Nepal-made finished paper when Charles Cowan remanufactured it in Britain.

`material identity retained`
while
`process history lost`.

Mechanisms:

- `PROCESS_HISTORY_EMBODIED_IN_SHEET`
- `MATERIAL_IDENTITY_DOES_NOT_PRESERVE_PROCESS_PROPERTIES`
- `METROPOLITAN_REMANUFACTURE_FAILURE_DESPITE_EXPERTISE`.

### Darjeeling 1841

After material transfer proved insufficient, skilled Nepali papermakers were moved. Iron vessels, washing, bleaching and strength/whiteness trade-offs reveal calibrated practice exceeding a printed recipe.

Mechanisms:

- `EMBODIED_PROCESS_TRANSFER`
- `TACIT_CRAFT_KNOWLEDGE_EXCEEDS_PRINTED_RECIPE`
- `WHITENESS_STRENGTH_TRADEOFF`.

### Serampore

Long-fibre/mucilaginous properties useful in hand-sheet manufacture became problems at a mechanised formation interface.

Mechanism:

`AFFORDANCE_REVERSAL`.

## 2. Stock time and working-format standardization remain series-level

RAS 022 architectural drawings preserve different watermark years inside one c.1844 programme, including at least 1821, 1837 and 1841.

The dominant numbered working format is extraordinarily stable:

- 47 numbered drawings currently recovered with paper dimensions;
- 43 occupy the long-format family;
- mean about `30.10 × 48.54 cm`;
- sample SD about `0.21 × 0.25 cm`.

23 long sheets have dated-watermark statements:

- 22 dated 1841;
- 1 dated 1837;
- mean about `30.09 × 48.56 cm`.

20 long sheets lack a dated-watermark statement yet have essentially the same mean dimensions.

Therefore:

`format standardization != stock homogeneity`

and

`catalogue watermark silence != another format class`.

The dominant c.30 × 48.5 cm format remains a very high dimensional/period-trade candidate for **half of a Royal 19 × 24 inch drawing sheet**, because Royal 19 × 24 is directly attested as a live commercial drawing-paper size in 1843.

Do not identify maker from size.

Mechanisms:

- `WORKING_FORMAT_STANDARDIZATION_IS_SERIES_LEVEL`
- `PARENT_SHEET_TO_WORKING_UNIT_CONVERSION`
- `CUTTING_IS_PAPER_ALLOCATION`
- `SIZE_CLASS_IDENTITY_DOES_NOT_EQUAL_MAKER_IDENTITY`
- `STOCK_TIME_IS_SERIES_LEVEL_NOT_OUTLIER`.

## 3. Preliminary and finished visual work can occupy different paper states

RAS preliminary/reference sheets are shorter and more heterogeneous than the formal numbered programme.

BL Lawrence Collection `Add Or 5229–5255` explicitly identifies multiple RAS sheets as original pencil sketches for finished drawings on a newly allocated support:

- RAS 022.058 -> BL Add Or 5233;
- RAS 022.055 -> Add Or 5244;
- RAS 022.054 -> Add Or 5246;
- RAS 022.052 -> Add Or 5251.

The Lawrence group is normally on English paper watermarked 1840–42 and survives in a Nepalese-paper folder; individual Nepalese-paper finished sheets also occur.

Material operation:

`working sketch support`
-> `visual transfer/copying`
-> `finished support`
-> `folder/container`.

Mechanisms:

- `WORKING_SKETCH_IS_COPY_INFRASTRUCTURE`
- `FINISHING_CAN_TRIGGER_SUPPORT_SUBSTITUTION`
- `ONE_VISUAL_OBJECT_CAN_HAVE_MULTIPLE_PAPER_STATES`
- `PAPER_ORIGIN_DOES_NOT_FIX_FUNCTION`.

Measurement guardrail remains strict: paper dimension, image/frame dimension, mount dimension and bound-leaf dimension are different fields.

## 4. Wave 64: local Nepali paper is a quantified production input

`MSS EUR HODGSON/12` reveals a Residency-managed paper/bookmaking economy.

### 1843 f.117

Catalogue: bill to Nathaniel Wallich for Nepali paper and book-binding material, signed by Hodgson at Nepal Residency.

The bill itself is on **machine-made paper**.

Catalogue transcription includes:

- Nepali paper in `ḍhyāp` bundles;
- gloss `10 sheets in one bundle`;
- wax cloth;
- thread/twine;
- Nepali and Indian rupee accounting.

The literal catalogue quantity/rate/total arithmetic does not reconcile. Therefore:

`NORMALIZED_SHEET_QUANTITY = UNRESOLVED`.

Do not publish `10,120 sheets` unless the original bill is read directly.

### 1827 ff.128–130

Bills for manuscripts sent to Wallich include:

- local paper purchase;
- copying;
- binding;
- bundling;
- packaging;
- carpenter/labourer/porter costs;
- shipment to Calcutta.

The bills themselves use **Nepali handmade and machine-made paper**.

### Logistics scale

Other vol.12 records give manuscript bundles, packages, loose leaves and boxes, allowing a material sequence:

`paper stock`
-> `copied leaves`
-> `manuscript`
-> `bundle/package`
-> `box`
-> `shipment`
-> `institutional receipt`.

Mechanisms:

- `LOCAL_PAPER_PROCUREMENT_IS_QUANTIFIED`
- `PAPER_PURCHASE_IS_EMBEDDED_IN_BOOKMAKING_ECONOMY`
- `PACKAGING_IS_PART_OF_PAPER_SURVIVAL_TECHNOLOGY`
- `RESIDENCY_AS_ACCOUNTING_AND_PRODUCTION_NODE`
- `ACCOUNT_LAYOUT_IS_EVIDENCE`.

## 5. Wave 65: commerce knowledge is recopied through a heterogeneous paper ecology

The 1831 commerce investigation supplies the strongest current negative case against any language/support equation.

Controlled states:

- `vol.53/31` — Nepali trade/customs data collected by Hanumanta Sing; **mixed machine-made + Nepali handmade paper**;
- `vol.60/14` — Nepali shop lists; **Nepali handmade paper**; explicit cross-reference to English report states;
- `vol.53/24` — Nepali commercial-mart list; **Nepali handmade paper**;
- `vol.94/15` — Farsi Calcutta-price list; **machine-made paper**;
- `vol.96/19` — Farsi Calcutta-import list; **Nepali handmade paper**;
- `vol.14/18` — English draft; **machine-made paper of various types**;
- `vol.14/16` and `vol.6/5` — English fair/publication-facing copies; **machine-made paper**.

Therefore:

`support origin != language`

`source status != support class`

`machine-made != final English stage`

`same information domain can cross support class`.

A controlled direct material transform exists for the shop list:

`Nepali handmade source sheet`
-> `English machine-paper report family`.

The Farsi machine-paper and Farsi Nepali-paper Calcutta import lists remain a possible copy/abstract family; direction is unresolved.

Mechanisms:

- `RECOPYING_IS_MATERIAL_TRANSLATION`
- `PAPER_CHANGE_CAN_ACCOMPANY_LANGUAGE_CHANGE_AND_AGGREGATION`
- `SOURCE_COLLECTION_CAN_CONSUME_MULTIPLE_STOCKS`
- `PUBLICATION_FACING_BRANCH_CAN_STANDARDIZE_SUPPORT_CLASS_WITHOUT_STANDARDIZING_STOCK`
- `RESIDENCY_AS_MATERIAL_INFORMATION_CONVERSION_NODE`.

The 1874 printed import summary has not yielded a clear `paper/stationery` commodity entry. Treat this as `NOT_YET_DIRECTLY_IDENTIFIED`, not as absence of imported paper circulation.

## 6. Wave 66: local paper reaches Hodgson through research intermediaries as well as bills

Two vol.53 catalogue statements are unusually explicit about **support acquisition**.

### f.97 — 1831

- Nepali handmade paper;
- tenancy-regulation text;
- catalogue note: `paper acquired through Rajmansingh`.

### f.99 — 1830

- Nepali handmade paper;
- tax-free-land text;
- catalogue note: `paper acquired from a Chitrakar`.

Cambridge elsewhere documents `citrakār` as an information collector/writer and repeatedly suggests Hodgson's artist/assistant Rajman Singh as a likely referent in some cases. But the unnamed Chitrakar on f.99 must remain:

`POSSIBLY_RAJMAN_SINGH / NOT_PROVEN`.

The paper-history consequence is secure without resolving identity:

**local collaborators could mediate the support itself, not only information or images.**

Thus local supply requires at least:

1. `BILLED_PURCHASE`;
2. `COLLABORATOR_MEDIATED_ACQUISITION`;
3. `RESIDENCY_STOCK_ALLOCATION`.

Potential sequence:

`local manufacture`
-> `market/workshop/household/occupational stock`
-> `[bill OR collaborator-mediated acquisition]`
-> `Residency working stock`
-> `task allocation`.

The middle stages are unresolved.

Mechanisms:

- `LOCAL_PAPER_ACQUISITION_HAS_MULTIPLE_CHANNELS`
- `RESEARCH_INTERMEDIARY_CAN_BE_PAPER_SUPPLY_INTERMEDIARY`
- `PAPER_PROVENANCE_CAN_ATTACH_TO_PERSONAL_NETWORK`
- `SUPPORT_PROVENANCE_NOT_INFORMATION_PROVENANCE`.

Do not infer that Raj Man Singh made the paper, sold paper commercially, or supplied the RAS drawing stock.

## 7. Current paper ontology

Do not use `sheet` as an untyped category.

Minimum stages:

`manufactured/commercial parent sheet`
-> `stock-held sheet/bundle`
-> `cut/folded working unit`
-> `written/drawn/copying support`
-> `bound/mounted/packaged object`
-> `modern archival unit`.

Minimum provenance fields:

- `maker/mill or local manufacture state`;
- `support class`;
- `watermark/countermark`;
- `commercial size candidate`;
- `paper_acquisition_agent`;
- `acquisition_mode`;
- `stock age at use`;
- `cut/fold state`;
- `task/documentary operation`;
- `language`;
- `writer/copyist`;
- `information provenance`;
- `container/mount/binding state`;

Core rule:

`support provenance`, `information provenance`, and `writer identity` are independent variables.

## 9. Current strongest formulation

**Hodgson's research paper was made more than once. Fibre and manufacturing practice produced physical properties; merchants, local collaborators and Residency accounts moved sheets into working stocks; retained paper accumulated stock time; cutting and folding converted commercial or local stock into task-sized supports; lists, translations, sketches and fair copies repeatedly transferred information onto newly allocated sheets; and binding, packaging, shipment, mounting and later archival rebinding remade those paper objects again. The surviving collection therefore does not divide cleanly into Nepalese and European paper. It preserves a mixed supply-and-conversion ecology in which the same language can cross support classes, the same support class can serve several documentary stages, and local research assistants can mediate both knowledge and the paper on which that knowledge is written.**

## Current compression

The paper now follows recursive material/documentary versioning across six analytically connected operations:

1. `material/fibre/process -> sheet properties`;
2. `stock/workflow -> paper allocation`;
3. `classification problem -> documentary form -> aggregation scale`;
4. `working manuscript -> fair copy -> print -> manuscript-on-print -> serial/collected reprint -> copy-specific branching`;
5. `administrative submission -> parallel governance copies -> record-series branching`;
6. `custody/weeding/rebinding -> survival selection -> modern relation repair`.

Combined model:

`paper-object history = production process × stock/supply × documentary operation × aggregation scale × classification regime × version/copy state × institutional record channel × locus-specific revision × custody/survival`.

Central question:

**what survives when material, information, a document, a printed locus or a relation is repeatedly recopied, aggregated, redistributed, rebound and selected into another operational state?**

Wave 58 established that administrative knowledge was already classificatory before Hodgson's surviving 1831 thar/clan paper. Wave 59 adds that classification itself moved through an institutional copy architecture whose later archival survival selectively preserves one governance branch over others.

## F. Custody is now explicitly a version-selection mechanism

British Library's own series history says military-letter sets were maintained at both East India House and the Board of Control. After those institutions ended, duplicated records were extensively weeded. For incoming military letters before 1858, Board copies were retained in preference to the Company set.

Thus the current archive represents a selective survival state:

`parallel governance record branches`
-> `post-institutional duplicate weeding`
-> `preferential survival of Board-copy branch`.

This directly strengthens the article's archive recursion:

`administrative relation/copy ecology`
-> `institutional custody`
-> `selection/weeding/rebinding`
-> `apparent singular archival witness`.

Mechanisms:

- `CUSTODY_IS_VERSION_SELECTION`
- `WEEDING_COLLAPSES_PARALLEL_RECORD_BRANCHES`
- `ARCHIVAL_SURVIVAL_CAN_HIDE FORMER DUPLICATION`
- `RECORD_SERIES_ARCHITECTURE_IS VERSION HISTORY`.

The general L/MIL series also records later enclosure removal/rebinding and multiple-copy `originals`; do not project the post-1835 enclosure operation automatically onto the specific 1830 item.

## Current strongest formulation

**Hodgson's paper world belonged to a wider documentary ecology in which classification was repeatedly remade by form and copying. Military information moved from person-level records into abstracts and comparative tables; Hodgson reorganized related categories into thars, clans, origins and genealogical relations; administrative submissions generated parallel Company, Board and personal-paper branches; print reopened texts to marginal correction; and later archives selected, weeded, rebound and reconnected those branches. The surviving archive is therefore not the passive container of a finished classification but a selectively preserved material version graph of the operations that produced, transmitted and stabilized it.**

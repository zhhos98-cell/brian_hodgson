# Paper argument module — material branching through documentary conversion v0.2

Date: 2026-08-31

Status: article-facing revision after Waves 30–31 and `support_conversion_pairs_v0_2.json`. Supersedes v0.1 where v0.1 implied that support switch itself was the principal relation to explain.

## Revision in one sentence

The archive does not show a one-way movement from local handmade paper into machine paper. It shows **versioned material branching**: related information can be remade on a different support, retained on the same support, or generate several derivative states on different supports.

## The new object of explanation

Old formulation:

`knowledge conversion -> possible support switch`.

Better formulation:

`source state -> derivative documentary state(s)`

where each derivative has its own:

- language;
- actor/writer;
- degree of abstraction;
- format;
- correction state;
- binding state;
- next intended operation;
- paper stock.

The explanatory variable is therefore not `translation` in the abstract.

It is the making of a **particular derivative state**.

Working model:

`target support = source support × available stock × actor/workplace × derivative format × intended operation × custody/publication pathway`.

This remains a falsifiable working model, not a finished causal equation.

## 1. Support switch remains real

The strongest switch cases survive intact:

- Nepali salary source -> English machine-paper administrative abstract;
- Nepali Tarai contracts -> English machine-paper copies;
- Newar festival source -> English machine-paper notebook;
- nirkhi customs contract -> English machine-paper prices/wages register;
- confiscated-property list -> English machine-paper ethnographic working copy;
- local ethnographic source family -> English machine-paper `Hill tribes` synthesis;
- Nepalese chronicle/genealogical source -> English machine-paper historical synthesis;
- local royal-code source family -> English machine-paper comparative notebook.

These cases support a precise claim:

**some acts of documentary recoding also rematerialised the information onto a different paper regime.**

The change of support can accompany new capacities for:

- larger charts;
- pre-bound notebooks;
- marginal correction;
- comparative tabulation;
- fair copying;
- circulation to another readership;
- storage in an English analytical archive.

Mechanism:

`SUPPORT_SWITCH_AT_KNOWLEDGE_CONVERSION`.

## 2. Support continuity is equally important

Two explicit pairs now require a different mechanism.

### Birta

`vol.53/19` Nepali/Hindi source on Nepali handmade paper
-> `vol.7/21` English description on Nepali handmade paper.

### Siddhinarasimha Malla

local Nepali historical source on Nepali handmade paper
-> `vol.21/3` English translated/rewritten account on Nepali handmade paper.

The second derivative is in the rough hand of a non-native user of English, which raises an actor/workplace hypothesis: translation performed within a local assistant/writer production environment may sometimes remain in the local paper-stock regime.

That hypothesis requires handwriting and stock inspection before use in the article.

Mechanism:

`SUPPORT_CONTINUITY_THROUGH_KNOWLEDGE_CONVERSION`.

The methodological payoff is immediate. Language change is not sufficient to explain paper change.

## 3. The route case proves branching

The Kathmandu-to-Beijing route is the first near-controlled documentary comparison.

Source:

`vol.59/23`

- Nepali;
- Nepali handmade paper;
- mission route with stages and distances.

Derivative A:

`vol.2/3`

- English;
- imported thick paper;
- fair summary state.

Derivative B:

`vol.102/19`

- Urdu/Farsi;
- Nepali handmade paper;
- incomplete translation state.

Therefore:

`one information lineage`
-> `imported-paper fair English derivative`
AND
-> `country-paper Urdu/Farsi derivative`.

The source subject is held constant. Translation is present in both derivatives. Support diverges.

The next variables are derivative state and production pathway.

Mechanisms:

- `BRANCHED_DERIVATIVE_SUPPORT_STATES`
- `DERIVATIVE_STATE_SELECTS_SUPPORT_WITHIN_SHARED_INFORMATION_LINEAGE`.

## 4. Jumla revenue is an even cleaner branching experiment

Source:

`vol.59/38`

- detailed Jumla revenue/levies;
- Nepali;
- Nepali handmade paper.

Derivative A:

`vol.8/1`

- English;
- machine-made paper;
- 48.5 × 38 cm;
- chart form;
- written by Campbell.

Derivative B:

`vol.102/21`

- Farsi;
- Nepali handmade paper;
- very neat;
- chart form.

This case controls one variable that the route case did not: both derivatives are tabular/chart states.

Thus even:

`source -> chart`

does not uniquely select paper support.

The contrast instead points toward a package:

`language/readership + actor + chart scale + administrative use + stock access`.

The large English Campbell chart is especially important because its dimensions and named actor are already catalogued.

Mechanism:

`SAME_SOURCE_SAME_GENERAL_FORMAT_DIFFERENT_SUPPORT`.

This should become one of the first physical-inspection pairs.

## 5. Newar kings chronicle gives partial branching

`vol.55/9` is a Nepali handmade-paper chronicle.

It explicitly generates:

- `vol.17/3`, English chronicle/synthesis in a pre-bound notebook whose support is not stated in the current inventory.

This case matters methodologically because the missing material field cannot be filled by inference.

`pre-bound notebook != machine-made paper`.

The corpus must retain:

`relation secure / support unclassified`.

Mechanism:

`RELATION_SECURE_SUPPORT_STATE_MISSING`.

## 6. Material branching changes the article's language

Avoid:

`local source is translated onto imported paper`.

That sentence is true for several cases but wrong as a general model.

Better:

**Hodgson's archive preserved multiple material versions of related information. Translation, abstraction and synthesis could coincide with a move onto machine or imported paper, but other derivatives remained on Nepalese handmade stock, and the same source could branch into both outcomes. Paper selection therefore belonged to the production of a derivative documentary state rather than to language conversion alone.**

The difference is significant.

The first formulation makes paper a marker of a binary transition.

The second makes paper a variable inside a versioning system.

## 7. Relation to the manufacturing experiment

The article now has two unusually compatible forms of conversion evidence.

### Manufacture

`related fibre/material -> different production sequence -> different sheet properties`.

### Documentary work

`related information -> different derivative-production sequence -> different material/documentary state`.

The common question is not metaphorical `translation`.

It is operational:

**which properties and relations persist when something is made into another usable state?**

In the Cowan experiment, fibre identity persists while successful sheet properties do not.

In the documentary archive, informational relation can persist while language, format, writer, paper and intended use change independently.

This makes paper the empirical bridge between technological history and the history of documentary knowledge practices.

## 8. Catalogue drift becomes part of the method

The conversion corpus has exposed several internal Cambridge-reference problems:

- item numbers can disagree with folio ranges;
- the same folio range can sit under a different current item number than an older cross-reference implies;
- a source can identify an English `summary` while the target catalogue calls the object something broader;
- historical source references can begin a few folios inside the target's current item boundary.

Therefore relation identity must be stored as:

`volume + folio range + title/content relation + printed cross-reference + current item resolution`.

Do not normalize item-number discrepancies silently.

Mechanism:

`CATALOGUE_RELATION_REQUIRES_FOLIO_CONTROL`.

This is also historically useful: the modern inventory is another versioned relational surface layered onto Hodgson's own versioned papers.

## 9. Current evidential threshold

`support_conversion_pairs_v0_2.json` now codes 19 explicit relation families.

Its internal classes include:

- switch-dominant relations;
- continuity relations;
- full branching relations;
- partial branching with unknown target support;
- relation-secure/support-unclassified cases.

The corpus is **search-saturated rather than denominator-complete**.

That distinction must stay in the article notes. Search-engine retrieval can miss variant catalogue wording. No percentage of `translations that switch paper` should be published until the full inventory has been programmatically or manually exhausted.

Qualitative mechanism claims are already justified because they rest on explicit paired relations.

## Bottom line

The strongest paper-centred claim is now more exact than `support switch at translation`. Hodgson's papers constitute a **material versioning system**. Related information could be copied, translated, abstracted and synthesised into multiple derivative objects, and paper was one variable in the production of those versions. Some derivatives changed support, some retained it, and the same source could generate both outcomes. The paper question is therefore not `local or imported?` and not even `did translation change the support?`. It is: **what derivative state was being made, by whom, with which stock, for what next operation, and why did that state require or tolerate that particular paper?**

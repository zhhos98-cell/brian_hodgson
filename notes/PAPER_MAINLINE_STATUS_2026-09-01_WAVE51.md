# Hodgson paper mainline status — 2026-09-01 — Wave 51

Status: current paper-centred article mainline. Supersedes earlier status files for active work while retaining them as research history.

## Current governing question

How do paper objects retain, change, branch, recombine and become recoverable across manufacture, documentary work, print, storage, transfer, rebinding, annotation and later cataloguing?

## Combined operational model

`paper-object history = production history × stock history × work allocation × documentary versioning × print/copy branching × custody architecture × archival reclassification × witness-access state`.

Paper is not a symbolic proxy for `local/colonial`, language, discipline or writer nationality. It is an operational variable whose properties, dimensions, surfaces, identifiers and copy states condition what can be done next.

## D. Historical container relations and later rebinding

The 1864 India Office transfer repeatedly co-bundled source, translation, abstract and multilingual derivative states.

The 1921 India Office rebinding partly sorted documents by physical dimensions and explicitly separated some originals from their translations because their paper sizes differed.

Thus:

`1864 version-family proximity`
→ `1921 dimension-based bound-volume proximity`.

Mechanisms:

- `VERSION_FAMILY_CO_BUNDLING_AS_ARCHIVAL_DESIGN`
- `PAPER_DIMENSION_DRIVES_ARCHIVAL_REBINDING`
- `ARCHIVAL_REBINDING_BREAKS_VERSION_FAMILIES`.

Modern Cambridge cross-references digitally repair some relations broken by physical rebinding.
